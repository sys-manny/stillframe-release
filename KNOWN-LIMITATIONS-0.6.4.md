# Stillframe 0.6.4 — known limitations

- Beta software; application and setup are unsigned. Keep backups and review operations before changing files.
- Automatic browsing uses saved information and does not create missing thumbnails. Choose Generate thumbnails explicitly when needed. Browsing acceleration and further thumbnail/media-information improvements remain deferred.
- Idle CPU optimization and biometric/people features are deferred, not included in this release.
- Device, cloud-provider, codec, accessibility, high-DPI and large-library coverage is incomplete. Focused generated-file checks do not guarantee every system or collection.
- No fresh-PC installation or owner-media acceptance is claimed for this publication. Screenshots use generated verification files; installer screenshots are no-install previews.
- Volume supports 0–150%, with 100% as the fresh default. Set 100% or lower before returning to 0.6.3, whose validator rejects higher saved values. Software volume readback does not establish hardware loudness or sound quality.
- Portable copies may share Windows-user preferences and caches. Use backups; an additional extraction is not automatically an isolated profile.
- Existing on-demand previews can be browsed without requesting originals. Original-file access remains subject to consent, eligibility checks and provider behavior; provider-specific outcomes are not universally verified.
- Library packages carry metadata, not original media. Missing/conflicting records or unresolved transactions can block an operation or reconnection. Preserve retained evidence rather than deleting it to bypass a refusal.

Report issues at https://github.com/sys-manny/stillframe-release/issues with version 0.6.4, steps, expected/actual behavior and exact errors. Do not upload private media or personal information.
